# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- Added configurable regular, initial, and final adrenaline gains for Tasty Mead.
- Added a configurable multiplier for positive adrenaline gains from other sources while Tasty Mead is active. The mod's own potion gains are excluded.

## 1.0.0

- Initial release: Tasty Mead grants adrenaline over the duration of its effect.
