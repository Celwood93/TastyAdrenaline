# Tasty Adrenaline

Tasty Adrenaline is a small mod that adds a tiny adrenaline gain to Tasty Mead. This is intended to keep adrenaline from decaying at key times, so you can get your trinket to activate!

## Effect

- Drinking Tasty Mead applies its normal status effect.
- While Tasty Mead is active, gain 1 adrenaline instantly and every 2 seconds.
- The adrenaline effect lasts 10 seconds and provides 6 adrenaline total.

## Installation

Install these dependencies through your preferred Valheim mod manager:

- BepInExPack Valheim
- Jotunn

Place `TastyAdrenaline.dll` in your Valheim `BepInEx/plugins` folder, then launch the game.
